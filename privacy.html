Privacy

How the Career Hub uses your information. Last updated 3 November 2026.

Who runs this site

The MSSW Career Hub is run by the placement team at Madras School of Social Work, Chennai, to help students and alumni with placements, preparation and staying connected.

What we collect

  • When you sign in: your name and email address. If you use "Sign in with Google", Google shares only your name and email with us. We never see your password.
  • What you tell us: your role (student, alumni or faculty), programme and batch.
  • What you choose to share: a profile photo, placement journeys, community posts, questions, openings and resources.
  • Basic activity: when you last visited, to show how many people are taking part.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and show you what your role can see. For example, this season's drives are only for @mssw.in accounts.
  • To show your name, photo and posts next to what you share.
  • To give the placement team simple participation counts.

We do not sell your information, show ads, or share it with anyone outside the MSSW placement team.

What others can see

Your name, photo and anything you post are visible to other visitors, as shown on the site. Your email address is never shown publicly. Only the placement team can see the member list.

Where it is stored

Your information is kept in the placement team's Google Workspace (Google Sheets and Google Drive) at Madras School of Social Work.

Your choices

You can remove your photo, or hide your own posts, from your profile at any time. To have your profile and everything you shared deleted, email the placement team at umashaan25@mssw.in and we will remove it.
